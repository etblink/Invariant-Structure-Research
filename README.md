# Invariant Structure Research

**Status:** PARKED — preservation-first exploratory research space

Invariant Structure Research studies recurring mathematical and structural invariants across apparently distinct domains and projects.

The motivating observation is simple:

> The projects under comparison do not exist in separate realities. They are all embedded in the same global universe and therefore must ultimately be compatible with the same reality, even when they use different vocabularies, scales, representations, or methods.

That observation is **not** itself evidence that the projects share one mechanism, one mathematics, or one theory.

This repository exists to test that possibility carefully.

## Governing question

> Which mathematical or structural invariants recur across apparently distinct domains, and which apparent correspondences survive rigorous attempts to distinguish genuine shared structure from analogy or coincidence?

## Initial comparison set

The initial research space includes, but is not limited to:

- Nested Fibrational Cosmology (NFC)
- Physical Grammar Hypothesis (PGH)
- Foundational Convergence Program (FCP)
- Project Observatory
- Project Continuity Research (PCR)
- HiVenues
- Evidence-Based Market Methods (EBMM)
- Frames of Reality (FoR) — pre-inception
- Theological Foundations Program (TFP) — pre-inception

These projects are not presumed to be mutually validating.

They are being compared because they may instantiate recurring structures such as:

- symmetry and invariance;
- lattices, graphs, adjacency, and networks;
- local/global relationships;
- hierarchy and nesting;
- state transitions;
- admissibility and constraints;
- canonical representation;
- persistence under transformation;
- equivalence versus identity;
- boundary conditions;
- information preservation;
- composition;
- reversibility and irreversibility;
- observer/representation relationships.

## Core epistemic boundary

**One reality does not imply one level of description.**

**One reality does not imply that evidence from one domain adjudicates another domain.**

**One reality does imply that genuine claims about overlapping domains cannot ultimately contradict each other without at least one claim, model, scope assumption, or interpretation being wrong.**

Accordingly, a recurring structure may be:

- `EXACT`
- `FORMAL_ANALOGUE`
- `POSSIBLE_BRIDGE`
- `SUPERFICIAL_SIMILARITY`
- `NOT_ESTABLISHED`

No stronger category may be inferred merely from shared terminology.

## Bridge test

Every proposed cross-domain relationship should identify four things:

1. **Objects** — what exactly corresponds to what?
2. **Mapping** — what transformation, relation, or construction connects them?
3. **Invariants** — what survives the mapping?
4. **Falsification** — what would show that the apparent correspondence is not substantive?

A useful analogy is not automatically an isomorphism.
An isomorphism is not automatically a shared mechanism.
A shared mechanism is not automatically a causal bridge.
A causal bridge in one regime is not automatically universal.

## Why this repository exists

This repository was created first as a **durable preservation and investigation space**.

It does not yet establish:

- a new unified theory;
- that NFC is an underlying theory of the other projects;
- that PGH, FCP, PCR, Observatory, HiVenues, EBMM, Frames of Reality, or TFP share one mechanism;
- that recurring mathematics implies recurring physics;
- that interdisciplinary resemblance is causal;
- or that a new autonomous long-running research program is warranted.

The immediate purpose is to preserve the emerging cross-project structural hypothesis outside any single conversation and then subject it to disciplined comparison.

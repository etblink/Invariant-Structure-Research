# Open Questions 0.1.2

**Status:** initial research queue  
**Rule:** questions are not claims.

## A. Foundational questions

1. Is there a useful formal meaning of "same global universe" for this research beyond the minimal consistency claim that overlapping true descriptions cannot ultimately contradict one another?

2. Which recurring structures are consequences of very general mathematics and therefore unsurprising across domains, versus genuinely diagnostic correspondences?

3. How should the research distinguish:
   - shared mathematical vocabulary;
   - shared abstract structure;
   - isomorphism;
   - reduction;
   - shared generative mechanism;
   - shared physical mechanism;
   - causal connection?

4. Can "structured multiplicity constrained by invariant relations" be made precise enough to risk falsification, or is it too general to be scientifically useful?

5. Is "representation versus invariant reality" a unifying research theme, or merely a broad philosophical description applicable to almost anything?

## B. Crystallography / cryptography bridge

1. Which structures used in crystallography and modern cryptography are literally the same mathematical objects?

2. Which similarities arise from common mathematical ancestry rather than direct historical influence?

3. Where do lattice, symmetry-group, reciprocal-space, coding, Fourier, or error/noise concepts genuinely intersect?

4. Can any such bridge teach us something operationally useful about the existing projects, or is it only an interdisciplinary precedent?

5. What would falsify an attempted crystallography ↔ cryptography analogy in a specific case?

## C. NFC ↔ PGH

1. What are the exact formal objects in NFC that the phrase "different grammars of the spine" is intended to describe?

2. What are the exact formal objects in PGH?

3. Is there an explicit mapping between any NFC structures and PGH grammar structures?

4. Are there invariants preserved under that mapping?

5. Does the mapping generate any result that was not already built into the descriptions?

6. What observations or derivations would show that NFC and PGH are merely conceptually related rather than formally related?

## D. NFC / PGH ↔ crystallographic structure

1. Which NFC crystallographic claims are mathematically crystallographic in the standard sense, rather than metaphorically so?

2. Do space groups, lattices, adjacency structures, reciprocal structures, defects, or symmetry breaking appear explicitly?

3. Which quantities are invariant under the relevant transformations?

4. Are any proposed physical consequences independently testable?

## E. FCP as comparison discipline

1. Can FCP's equal-standard methodology be adapted to compare cross-domain structural claims without importing FCP's own framework assumptions?

2. What is the correct null hypothesis for a proposed structural bridge?

3. How should negative correspondence results be preserved?

4. Can bridge classifications be scored or audited without turning them into a superficial taxonomy?

## F. PCR / Observatory ↔ mathematical structure

1. Which PCR concepts are implemented as actual graphs, partial orders, state machines, type systems, logical constraints, or cryptographic structures?

2. Which Observatory concepts are merely software conventions and which instantiate reusable mathematical structures?

3. Is project-state reconstruction formally comparable to state estimation in other fields, or only metaphorically?

4. Can context drift be represented as divergence between an agent's internal representation and a canonical or evidence-constrained external state?

5. What invariant, if any, defines "the same project" across successors?

## G. HiVenues ↔ invariant structure

1. Which HiVenues invariants are explicit in the architecture?

2. How do Studio, Preview, deployed public state, rollback state, and canonical host state relate formally?

3. Is the host-first interface best understood as a representation layer over invariant authority/state?

4. Which Hive cryptographic or authority structures are genuinely relevant to the structural crosswalk?

5. Are any similarities to PCR authority/state architecture caused by shared implementation needs rather than a deeper structural principle?

## H. Evidence-Based Market Methods ↔ invariant structure

1. When EBMM asks whether a market effect survives across instruments, periods, regimes, costs, and benchmarks, is that usefully understood as an empirical invariance test, or is "invariance" too strong a mathematical term?

2. Can the project's distinction among **evidence**, **synthesis**, and **decision** be mapped formally to similar separation layers in FCP, PCR, Observatory, or HiVenues?

3. Is `STATE.yaml` as sole canonical mutable state structurally comparable to canonical state constructs in PCR, Observatory, or HiVenues, or is the similarity merely an implementation convention?

4. Does EBMM's classification by information source or economic mechanism provide a useful example of quotienting many surface labels into fewer structurally meaningful classes?

5. Can robustness across market regimes be formalized as persistence under transformation without hiding the fact that market regimes are empirical and historically contingent?

6. Are benchmark-relative performance and opportunity-cost comparison structurally comparable to equal-standard comparison in FCP?

7. Do preregistered holdout boundaries in EBMM instantiate the same general anti-self-deception structure as preregistration and frozen gates elsewhere in the portfolio?

8. Can the research-to-capital boundary be compared to authority/execution boundaries in PCR and HiVenues without conflating financial risk with software or governance authority?

9. Which apparent EBMM bridges disappear once transaction costs, multiple testing, selection bias, and implementation constraints are included?

## I. Frames of Reality ↔ invariant structure

1. If the game eventually implements an explicit underlying world state, what parts of that state are truly invariant under changes of observer, faction, information access, or narrative framing?

2. Can character beliefs be modeled as representations of world state rather than as mere dialogue flags?

3. What transformations map world events into faction-specific or character-specific observations?

4. Which information losses are intentional design constraints and which are artifacts of implementation?

5. Can misinformation, rumor, propaganda, and correction be represented as transformations on information rather than only scripted story beats?

6. What would distinguish a substantive structural model of frame-versus-reality from a thematic use of those words?

7. Could the game provide a controlled synthetic domain for testing observer/representation ideas without falsely implying that the game's mechanics prove anything about the real universe?

8. Which moral or theological structures, if any, become actual game-system invariants rather than authorial themes?

## J. Theological Foundations Program ↔ invariant structure

1. Which theological structures are genuinely formal enough for cross-domain comparison?

2. Can textual, historical, interpretive, philosophical, doctrinal, empirical, and faith claims be represented as distinct layers without implying that they are reducible to one formal system?

3. Are canon formation, doctrinal development, and interpretive transmission useful examples of persistence and transformation, or is that vocabulary too generic?

4. Can competing theological frameworks be compared under an equal-standard discipline without assuming they share the same authority structure?

5. What, if anything, remains invariant across translation, historical development, denomination, or interpretive school?

6. How should the research distinguish structural coherence inside a theological system from truth about reality?

7. Could concepts such as unity/plurality, identity/distinction, relation, hierarchy, or incarnation create valid formal bridges to other domains, or would those comparisons be mostly verbal?

8. What would falsify a proposed theology-to-physics, theology-to-information, or theology-to-project-structure bridge?

9. How should personal or communal faith commitments be represented without treating them either as irrelevant noise or as publicly decisive evidence?

10. Which theological claims overlap enough with historical or empirical domains to fall under the common-reality consistency requirement in a directly testable way?

## K. Cross-project methodology

1. Should pairwise bridges be studied before any attempt at a global crosswalk?

2. How can multiple-comparison / pattern-mining bias be controlled when many projects and many structures are available?

3. Should candidate mappings be preregistered before inspecting the target project in detail?

4. When is external literature required to establish that a structure is standard rather than project-specific?

5. What evidence threshold should promote `POSSIBLE_BRIDGE` to `FORMAL_ANALOGUE` or `EXACT`?

6. Should some categories be directional rather than symmetric?

7. How should scale differences be represented when the same mathematics appears at radically different levels of description?

8. How should the method distinguish a true invariant from a criterion that merely asks for robustness across perturbations?

## L. Program-boundary questions

1. Does this repository remain a lightweight preservation/research space, or eventually earn status as an autonomous research program?

2. What result would justify expanding it?

3. What result would justify parking or closing it?

4. How can it avoid becoming another self-expanding support project?

5. Which existing project should benefit first from a validated structural bridge?

## M. Immediate next question

The highest-value next move is not to add more candidate similarities.

It is to choose one apparently strong bridge and force it through the full test:

> **Objects → Mapping → Invariants → Falsification**

The resulting failure or success should determine how the method evolves.

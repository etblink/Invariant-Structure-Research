# ISR Parking Boundary 0.1.0

**Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Status:** PARKED BY DESIGN

## Purpose

Invariant Structure Research is intentionally parked before further cross-project extraction involving the Theological Foundations Program.

The reason is methodological independence, not abandonment.

## Rationale

TFP is currently pre-inception and has only recently entered the portfolio.

Continuing ISR while simultaneously designing TFP would create a serious contamination risk:

- ISR could shape which concepts TFP formalizes;
- later ISR work could then “rediscover” structures that ISR itself helped introduce;
- this would weaken any claim of independent recurrence across domains.

Accordingly:

> ISR knows that TFP exists, but ISR should stop influencing TFP's development during the independence interval.

## Independence interval

During this interval:

- do not extend the ISR crosswalk using new TFP material;
- do not redesign TFP to fit ISR categories;
- do not search TFP for invariants, bridges, or correspondences;
- do not promote theology-specific concepts because they resemble structures in NFC, PGH, FCP, PCR, Observatory, HiVenues, EBMM, or Frames of Reality;
- allow TFP to develop theology-native vocabulary, problems, methods, evidence distinctions, and failure modes.

## Contamination accounting

TFP is **not fully PRE-ISR**, because ISR concepts were already discussed before this boundary was established.

Future structural evidence should therefore distinguish:

- `PRE-ISR` — structures demonstrably developed before ISR awareness;
- `ISR-AWARE-SEED` — structures present in the initial TFP seed after limited ISR exposure;
- `INDEPENDENCE-INTERVAL` — structures that emerge while ISR is parked and not used to guide TFP;
- `POST-REOPEN` — structures developed after ISR resumes.

The `INDEPENDENCE-INTERVAL` category may provide stronger evidence of spontaneous recurrence than structures deliberately introduced through ISR.

## Reactivation condition

ISR should not resume substantive TFP comparison merely because new theology material exists.

A later explicit decision should identify a meaningful TFP maturity checkpoint and freeze the theology state to be compared before ISR reopens.
